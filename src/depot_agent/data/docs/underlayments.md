# Roofing Underlayments

## StormGuard Synthetic Underlayment (UND-001)
Coverage: 10 squares (1,000 sq ft) per roll.
Roll weight: 28 lb.
UV exposure: can be left exposed for up to 180 days before shingles are installed.
Surface: textured and slip-resistant, safer to walk on than felt.

## #30 Felt Underlayment (UND-002)
Coverage: 2 squares (200 sq ft) per roll.
Roll weight: 56 lb.
UV exposure: cover within 7 days. Felt wrinkles and degrades when it is wet and exposed.

## Choosing between them
Synthetic underlayment costs more per roll but covers five times the area, weighs much less per square and tolerates far longer exposure. Felt suits small, quick jobs.
