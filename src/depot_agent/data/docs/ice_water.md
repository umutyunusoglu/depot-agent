# IceBlock Ice & Water Shield (ICE-001)
Self-adhering rubberised asphalt membrane for leak-prone areas of the roof.

## Coverage
2 squares (200 sq ft) per roll.

## Where to use it
Eaves, valleys, around chimneys, skylights and other roof penetrations. Many building codes require it at the eaves in areas where ice dams are common.

## Installation
Minimum application temperature: 40°F (4°C). Apply to a clean, dry roof deck. Do not leave exposed for more than 30 days.
