# Northline Building Supply - Customer Policies

## Returns
Unopened, undamaged products in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery with the original invoice. A 15% restocking fee applies.
Opened products, cut-to-length items and special orders cannot be returned.

## Delivery
Free delivery on orders over $2,500 within 50 miles of a Northline warehouse. Below that, a flat $95 delivery fee applies.
Roof-top delivery (placing bundles directly on the roof) costs an extra $3 per bundle.

## Pickup
Orders placed before 11:00 am are ready for same-day pickup at the warehouse.

## Pricing
Listed prices are per unit (bundle, roll, box, piece or bag) and exclude sales tax.
